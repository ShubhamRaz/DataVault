==================================================
1. PRODUCT VISION
==================================================

DataVault is a privacy-first AI collaboration marketplace.

Organizations have valuable data but cannot directly share raw data because of:

- privacy regulations
- security concerns
- competitive sensitivity
- data ownership
- legal/compliance restrictions
- data siloing

DataVault allows multiple organizations to collaboratively train AI models without transferring their raw datasets to a central server.

Core principle:

RAW DATA NEVER LEAVES THE DATA OWNER.

Instead:

1. A global model is created.
2. The global model is distributed to participating organizations.
3. Each organization trains the model locally.
4. Only model updates are produced.
5. Updates are privacy-protected/encrypted.
6. A secure aggregation layer combines updates.
7. A new global model is generated.
8. Contribution metrics are recorded.
9. Blockchain records contribution proofs.
10. Smart-contract logic calculates rewards.
11. Participants receive DataVault tokens/reward points.
12. Dashboards show model performance, privacy status, contributions, rewards, and audit events.

The hackathon MVP should demonstrate this concept using synthetic datasets.
